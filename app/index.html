Roadmap to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

One playthrough is enough. Published roadmaps rate the platinum about 4/10 for difficulty and roughly 200 hours, with the time going into the 100% checklist rather than hard trophies. There are no online trophies and no difficulty settings to worry about. Two standing rules: never use cheats (they disable trophies and saving) and keep manual saves at chapter starts and ends, because the game has no mission replay. Roadmap and missable flags below are built from the official trophy descriptions, cross-checked against the sources on the Sources tab.

Missable watchlist — read before you play

Honest tiers, not alarm bells. Possibly missable means a guide with a platinum in hand flags a real risk; watch means availability pauses that resolve later. Everything else in the list cannot be missed.

Sources & how this guide was built

Every trophy guide in the Ledger names its sources beside the guide itself. Official names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them, via the two list sources below; every method, count and warning is rewritten in our own words from the guide sources — cited, never copied. Where sources disagreed (for example on how hard the platinum is), the Ledger follows the official list for grades and says so in the trophy's guide. Links open only when you follow them; the app itself loads nothing from the network and works fully offline.

Save & restore your progress

Progress is stored in this browser (localStorage) as you tick trophies off. Use Export on the Tracker tab to download it as a file, and Import to restore it on any machine — handy before switching browsers. Your platinum plan, in one file.
