    The road to Vice Got a Price!

    Six phases, in the order that makes the platinum fastest. The story first, then the side stories, then the activities, the robberies, the world grind — and the oddities and the 100% checklist last. Each phase shows its own progress, so the whole path reads at a glance.

    Built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals — the platinum is long, not hard. Guides put it at roughly 200 hours.

      Missable trophies

      There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus in GTA VI, so the game gives you 15 manual save slots for a reason. Keep one save from the start of every chapter and one from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest. Only one trophy is known to be missable right now; one more strand is time-sensitive. Everything else stays available after the credits.
