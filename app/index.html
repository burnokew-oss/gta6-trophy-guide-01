Watch out: one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze) — meditate at every spot across Leonida. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward it and disappears once that chapter ends. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission.

Also time-sensitive: Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 (it counts toward Strangers & Freaks). Do it the moment it appears. Everything else stays available after the credits.

Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours

Six phases, in order. The story first (with your chapter saves), then side stories, robberies, activities and collections, and finally the 100% checklist. Roughly 200 hours in total, difficulty about 4/10 — long, not hard. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Cheats should switch off trophies and saving, so keep them off your trophy save.
